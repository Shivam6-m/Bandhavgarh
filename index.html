01 · WILDLIFE EXPERIENCE

Jungle Safari in Bandhavgarh National Park

Explore the natural beauty of Bandhavgarh National Park in Madhya Pradesh, one of India's well-known destinations for tiger safaris and wildlife experiences. Discover beautiful forests, grasslands, hills and diverse wildlife in its natural habitat.

Discover Bandhavgarh's Wildlife

Bandhavgarh is famous for the Bengal tiger. Visitors may also encounter leopards, deer, wild boar, sloth bears and a variety of bird species. Wildlife sightings depend on natural conditions and are never guaranteed.

Explore the Safari Zones

The main core safari zones include Tala, Magadhi and Khitauli. Buffer safari areas include Dhamokhar, Johila and Panpatha. Each area offers its own forest landscapes and wildlife habitats.

Morning & Evening Safari

Safari trips generally operate in morning and afternoon sessions. Exact timings depend on the season and forest department rules. Advance planning is recommended, especially during busy travel periods.

Safari Booking Assistance

Contact Bandhavgarh Forest Galaxy to enquire about safari dates, permit availability, zones, accommodation and taxi arrangements. Safari permits are subject to availability and must be confirmed through the authorised booking process.
